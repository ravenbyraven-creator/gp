# Gorilla Position — Vision

## What this app is

Gorilla Position is a **companion app for WWE 2K26 Universe Mode**.

Think of it as the **ChatGPT for your Universe Mode save**. You play on your console (PlayStation), and you use Gorilla Position on the side — at night, on your phone, on a laptop, anywhere away from the game — to talk through your shows, plan your storylines, and shape what happens next on your roster.

It is a **planning and conversation tool**, not a second copy of the game.

---

## Who it's for

One person at a time: a Universe Mode booker who wants a creative partner outside the game.

You are the head of creative. The app is your writers' room.

---

## The golden rule

**If WWE 2K26 already does it, the app does NOT do it.**

The game already handles:

- Match cards and match results
- Match types, finishers, move sets
- Stats, ratings, attributes
- Title reigns and championship trees as a database
- Calendar progression
- Drafts, contracts, injuries (game-side mechanics)

Duplicating any of that is wasted effort and clutter. The app has no business tracking those things — they live where you actually play.

---

## What the app focuses on

The stuff the game is **bad at** or doesn't do at all:

1. **Talking through your storylines** — a chat with an AI that knows your roster, your shows, and the angles you have running. You can workshop ideas, ask "what should Cody do after WrestleMania?", or sketch out the next three months of TV.
2. **Capturing the creative side of your save** — the storylines, the rivalries, the character arcs, the long-term direction of each superstar. The narrative your save has, that no game UI shows you.
3. **Your roster as you see it** — not the game's database, but your version: which superstars matter to you right now, what show they're on, what they're doing creatively, who they're feuding with.
4. **Your shows as creative brands** — RAW, SmackDown, and any custom brand you create, each with their own identity, look, and ongoing storylines.
5. **A place to think** — somewhere to jot, plan, and have a conversation about the save, the way you would with a friend who also watches wrestling.

---

## How you actually use it

A normal week looks like this:

- You play a few hours of Universe Mode on PS5 in the evening.
- Later that night, in bed or on the couch, you open Gorilla Position.
- You tell the app what just happened in-game, or you ask it to help you figure out what should happen next.
- You update your roster, log a new rivalry, sketch out a Premium Live Event card from a story angle.
- Next time you boot up 2K26, you already know what you want to book.

The app is the **planning layer that wraps around the game**. The game is the simulator; the app is the writers' room.

---

## What's already built

- **Creative Desk** — chat with your AI creative team about anything related to your save. Pre-loaded prompts to get going.
- **Roster** — your superstars, organized by show, with profile pictures, alignment, gender, notes, and free-agent status. Card view for visuals, list view for fast scanning. Show selection automatically applies the right brand color.
- **Shows** — RAW, SmackDown, and any custom show you want to create, each with its own art and brand identity that flows through the rest of the app.
- **Rivalries** — track your active feuds and the storylines behind them.
- **Weekly Schedule** — a simple Mon–Sun view of what's airing this week in your save.
- **News Ticker** — flavor that surfaces your storylines as breaking news headlines.
- **Branding** — Gorilla Position name, custom fonts, dark UI tuned to feel like a backstage tool, not a game menu.

---

## What's NOT in scope

These are deliberate exclusions to keep the app focused:

- Match builders, match cards, match results trackers
- Move set / finisher / signature databases
- Attribute or rating systems
- Title histories as a structured belt-by-belt database (the game shows you this; we only care about the *story* of a reign)
- Anything that requires you to re-enter data the game already has
- Multiplayer, social features, sharing saves
- Trying to replace or replicate the game's UI

If something feels like "I'd be inputting this twice," it doesn't belong here.

---

## Where this is heading

Future direction stays inside the same lane: **more ways to think about your save, not more ways to record it.**

Possible directions (not commitments):

- Deeper AI memory of your save's history so the chat actually remembers your storylines week to week.
- Storyline timelines — long-form arcs you can scroll through.
- Character bios that grow with your save.
- A "pitch" view where the AI proposes options and you pick one.
- Voice mode for hands-free planning.
- Mobile-first polish so the phone is the primary device.

Anything that doesn't pass the golden rule — *"would I just be re-typing what the game already shows me?"* — gets cut.
