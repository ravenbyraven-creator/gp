Read those filters. Two killers:

kind: "log" is filtered OUT of rivalryHistory. Your filed-to-rivalry chat saves are literally excluded from the "ongoing rivalries" the AI sees.
The "recentEvents" bucket that does include logs is a flat list of the last 5 log entries across the entire universe — with no rivalry tag, no participant info, no "this belongs to Cody vs Punk." Just text.
And look at activeRivalryMemories (line 408-431) — it pulls from useMemories(), a completely separate store that only gets populated by the AI's "Distill Memories" button. Your filed chat saves never end up in useMemories(). So when the AI checks "what do I know about this rivalry?" it's checking the wrong drawer.

Net result
You chat about Cody vs Punk
AI gives a great beat
You hit Save to Rivalry
Entry saved as kind=log, attached to rivalry
RivalryDetail UI shows it. Looks good.
Next question: 'what next for Cody vs Punk?'
buildBookerContext runs
Filters logs OUT of rivalryHistory
Pulls last 5 logs as flat 'recentEvents' with no rivalry context
activeRivalryMemories reads useMemories which YOUR save never touched
AI receives: 5 most recent storyline/show entries (none of them yours)
AI gives a beat that ignores everything you just filed
So you file something, the app says "Saved to rivalry history," and then the AI immediately forgets it. The rivalry-link is a decoration, not a signal.

That's exactly why I called Quick Book "rivalry-blind" — it's not just Quick Book, it's the whole AI context layer. The data structure to wire this up correctly already exists (Rivalry.historyEntryIds, useMemories, useChapters are all per-rivalry), but buildBookerContext ignores it.
